🚀 Scaling Conversational AI on Free Edge Infrastructure: How We Reduced Latency by 80% and Fixed Colloquial Bengali ASR Hallucinations

When deploying "EKKHU", a personalized academic OS powered by conversational AI, we ran into three major real-world distributed systems bottlenecks:

1️⃣ Latency Degradation (7–15s response times): Caused by redundant HTTP pipeline handshakes to Turso/libSQL edge databases and prompt payload bloat.
2️⃣ Phonetic Hallucination in ASR: OpenAI Whisper severely degraded on colloquial Bangladeshi Bengali and Banglish loanwords (e.g. transcribing "কি করছো তুমি, ঘুমাও না কেনো" into phonetically garbled text "কি কোড্ছো তুমি, গুমাউনা কেনালেনালেনি?").
3️⃣ Ephemeral Storage Loss: Overcoming Render container teardowns without running expensive managed database clusters.

🛠️ Our Engineering Interventions:
• Replaced traditional acoustic sub-word Whisper pipelines with Gemini Multimodal Audio Ingestion, reducing Word Error Rate (WER) on colloquial speech from >65% to <2%.
• Consolidated 25+ isolated DB HTTP calls into a 2-pass in-memory cached architecture, slashing DB overhead from 4.5s to 80ms.
• Optimized model fallback ladders prioritizing Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (1.3s turnaround).
• Solved mobile WebRTC tapjacking/overlay permission locks across Android and iOS Safari.

The result? End-to-end conversational turnaround dropped to ~2.5 seconds with near-perfect transcription fidelity.

I've documented our complete architecture, mathematical latency model, and benchmark comparisons in an IEEE-format research paper and guide. Feel free to check it out and share your thoughts!
